Flood Awareness & Safety

Be prepared. Stay informed. Stay safe. Learn how to protect yourself, your family, and your community from flooding.

5 Important Steps to Prepare for a Flood

Start preparing now to keep your family and home safe.

01

Stay Informed

Check weather forecasts and listen to official flood warnings. Know the flood risks in your area.

02

Prepare an Emergency Kit

Keep drinking water, food, medicines, a flashlight, and other important supplies ready.

03

Protect Important Items

Put important documents, money, and valuable belongings in waterproof bags and safe places.

04

Plan a Safe Route

Identify safe places on higher ground and learn how to reach them before flooding occurs.

05

Protect Your Home

Clear drains, move valuable items to higher places, and use suitable flood protection measures.

Build Your Emergency Kit

Prepare essential supplies in advance. Keep your emergency kit somewhere easy to reach.

  • Clean drinking water
  • Ready-to-eat food
  • First aid kit and medicines
  • Flashlight and extra batteries
  • Mobile phone and power bank
  • Important documents in waterproof bags
  • Extra clothes and hygiene items

Make a Family Emergency Plan

Talk with your family and decide what to do before a flood.

  1. Choose a safe meeting place for your family.
  2. Know the nearest evacuation locations.
  3. Decide how family members will contact each other.
  4. Help children, elderly people, and people with disabilities.
  5. Keep emergency contact numbers in a safe place.

⚠️ Important Safety Reminder

When flooding threatens, act early. Follow official emergency instructions. Never enter floodwater, even if it appears shallow. Your life is more important than your belongings.

What Is a Flood?

A flood occurs when water overflows onto land that is normally dry. Floods can happen because of heavy rainfall, overflowing rivers, storm surges, or drainage systems that cannot handle excess water. They can develop slowly or occur suddenly, putting lives, homes, infrastructure, and the environment at risk.


Our mission is to provide clear, reliable, and accessible flood safety information so everyone can make informed decisions and prepare for emergencies.

What Causes Floods?

Understanding the causes of flooding helps communities prepare for potential disasters.

Heavy Rainfall

Prolonged or intense rainfall can overwhelm rivers, drainage systems, and the ground's ability to absorb water.

Overflowing Rivers

Rivers may overflow their banks when water levels rise beyond their normal capacity.

Poor Drainage

Blocked drains, paved surfaces, and inadequate drainage infrastructure can increase urban flooding.

The Effects of Flooding

Floods can affect people, communities, economies, and the natural environment.

Property Damage

Floodwater can destroy homes, roads, bridges, schools, and essential infrastructure.

Threats to Human Life

Fast-moving water, drowning hazards, contaminated water, and electrical dangers can seriously threaten human safety.

Environmental Damage

Flooding can damage crops, erode soil, contaminate water sources, and disrupt wildlife habitats.

Flood Safety Tips

Knowing what to do before, during, and after a flood can reduce risks and help save lives.

🟢 Before a Flood

  • Monitor official weather forecasts and flood warnings.
  • Prepare an emergency kit with water, food, medicine, and a flashlight.
  • Know your evacuation routes and safe shelters.
  • Keep important documents in waterproof bags.
  • Plan how to contact and reunite with family members.

🔴 During a Flood

  • Follow official evacuation instructions immediately.
  • Move to higher ground when flooding threatens.
  • Never walk, swim, or drive through floodwater.
  • Stay away from fallen power lines and electrical equipment.
  • Keep listening to official emergency updates.

🔵 After a Flood

  • Return home only when authorities say it is safe.
  • Avoid floodwater that may contain sewage or chemicals.
  • Do not touch wet electrical equipment until it is safe.
  • Use safe drinking water and discard contaminated food.
  • Document property damage and seek assistance if needed.
